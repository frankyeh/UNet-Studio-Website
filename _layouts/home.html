Brain segmentation from a single template.

U-Net Studio trains a 3D U-Net from one template image with visual-perception augmentation, then skull-strips and segments brain MRI of humans, rhesus monkeys, marmosets, rats and mice. A standalone C++ program, sister tool to DSI Studio.
